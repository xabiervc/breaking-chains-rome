# Breaking Chains: Rome

Production bible for a deterministic, historically grounded open-world action-adventure set across the Roman Empire during AD 54–64.

## Core premise

Dama, a fictional Thracian-born enslaved blacksmith, escapes a Campanian estate after his sister Livia is sold into the imperial trafficking network. Over ten years he travels through the empire, builds a resistance network, dismantles the four power centers that protect the trade, and initiates the Great Fire of Rome on 18 July AD 64 as the canonical climax.

This is historical fiction. Real geography, institutions, languages, and historical events frame a fictional protagonist and fictional conspiracy.

## Fixed canon

- Title: `Breaking Chains: Rome`.
- Dates: AD 54–64.
- Protagonist: Dama, age 29 at the start.
- Final mission date: 18 July AD 64.
- Final outcome: the fictional trafficking network is destroyed; Roman slavery is not ended.
- Gameplay map: 300 square miles across ten connected regions.
- Voice policy: historically researched in-universe languages; modern subtitle options.
- Design policy: key narrative, routes, schedules, economy inputs, consequences, and final outcome are deterministic.

## Historical disclaimer

Ancient sources disagree about the Great Fire's cause and do not establish the game's explanation. The project presents Dama's secret role as historical fiction, not as a claim about what actually happened.

## Status

Second-edition production bible. The repository is documentation-first: canon and validation precede implementation.
