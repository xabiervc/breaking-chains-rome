# Breaking Chains: Rome

A deterministic, historically grounded open-world action-adventure set across a compressed but highly detailed representation of the Roman Empire during the reign of Nero, AD 54–64.

## Project overview

The player controls Dama, a fictional Thracian-born enslaved blacksmith who escapes a Campanian estate, travels through imperial territories, builds a resistance network, dismantles a major slave-trafficking conspiracy, rescues enslaved people, and ultimately initiates the Great Fire of Rome as a deliberate act of revenge and liberation.

This is historical fiction. Real places, institutions, travel routes, languages, and historical events provide the framework; Dama, the principal antagonists, the resistance network, and their secret role in the fire are fictional.

## Fixed creative pillars

- Four-act structure: escape, pursuit, revenge, and systemic attack.
- Fixed setting: Roman Empire, AD 54–64.
- Fixed protagonist: Dama, a Thracian-born enslaved blacksmith.
- Fixed climax: the Great Fire of Rome, beginning on 18 July AD 64.
- Fixed gameplay abstraction: 300 square miles across ten connected regions.
- Historically appropriate in-universe voice languages with modern subtitle options.
- Deterministic mission objectives, key schedules, routes, consequences, and final outcome.

## Historical-fiction disclaimer

The game uses real places, institutions, languages, and historical events, including the Great Fire of Rome in AD 64. The protagonist, principal antagonists, resistance network, and their direct involvement in the fire are fictional. Ancient sources disagree about the fire's cause; this project presents one fictional interpretation.
