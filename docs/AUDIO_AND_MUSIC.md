# Audio and Music

## Voice direction

Use historically researched language coaching, region-specific performance direction, and consistent subtitle metadata. Avoid caricatured accents and invented “ancient” pronunciation.

## Music

Original score only. Use period-inspired instrumentation without claiming certainty about exact ancient performance practice. Musical identity evolves from isolated metal, breath, and low strings in Act I to layered regional instruments and collective voices in Act IV.

## Sound motifs

- Iron and chain: ownership and labor.
- Water: movement, survival, and evacuation.
- Bells: warning, authority, and memory.
- Fire: destruction, evidence, and transformation.

## Accessibility

Provide separate controls for dialogue, ambience, music, subtitles, speaker labels, translation indicators, and important sound cues.
