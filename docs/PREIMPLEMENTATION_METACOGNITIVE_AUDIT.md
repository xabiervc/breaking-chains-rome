# Preimplementation Metacognitive Audit

## Decompose

The Level-A question was divided into identity, gameplay, narrative, production, accessibility, traceability, and honest closure.

## Solve with confidence

- Identity: 0.93 — core experience, audience-facing distinction, scope, and anti-goals are defined.
- Gameplay: 0.88 — loop, variables, approaches, costs, failure recovery, and slice are specified; feel and balance remain hypotheses.
- Narrative: 0.91 — arc, chronology, character agency, canon, and system consequences are specified.
- Production: 0.89 — slice, content scope, assets, audio, localization, technical plans, and gates are specified.
- Accessibility: 0.86 — measurable requirements and test coverage are specified; actual player validation remains open.
- Traceability: 0.84 — canonical matrix links pillars to mechanics, scenes, state, UI, tests, and acceptance; full campaign row coverage remains a production task.
- Honest closure: 0.96 — verified facts, targets, hypotheses, accepted risks, and open evidence are explicitly separated.

## Verify

Checks performed against the visible repository structure:

- Canonical source and status index exist.
- Operational vertical slice exists.
- Traceability matrix exists.
- Open evidence register exists.
- Accessibility, technical, save, localization, research, and QA plans exist.
- Legacy/final naming is explicitly prevented from overriding canonical documents.

Limitations: this audit does not execute CI, inspect every document body recursively, compile Unreal, measure runtime performance, or conduct human playtests and specialist reviews.

## Synthesize

Weighted documentary readiness confidence: 0.89.

The project meets the documentary Level-A threshold for controlled vertical-slice implementation. It does not meet the evidence threshold for production completion or award readiness.

## Reflect

No component is below 0.80 at the documentary level. The lowest-confidence areas are traceability coverage and accessibility validation; they are therefore explicit implementation/playtest gates rather than hidden assumptions.
