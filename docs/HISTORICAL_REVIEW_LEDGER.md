# Historical Review Ledger

## Evidence states

- `PUBLISHED_RESEARCH`: source located and claim classified.
- `INTERNAL_DESIGN_REVIEW`: team has applied the research.
- `SPECIALIST_REVIEW_PENDING`: external qualified review not yet completed.
- `SPECIALIST_REVIEW_COMPLETE`: written review exists and affected content was updated.

## Current status

Roman slavery, urbanism, the Great Fire, travel, material culture, languages, sensitivity, art, and audio have published research support and internal classification in `docs/research/`.

All external specialist reviews remain `SPECIALIST_REVIEW_PENDING` until named reviewers provide written feedback. No document may describe them as complete before that evidence exists.

## Required record per claim

Claim ID, classification, source, confidence, affected mission/asset, reviewer, review date, recommendation, change made, regression test, and sign-off location.
