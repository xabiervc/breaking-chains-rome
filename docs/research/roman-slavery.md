# Roman Slavery

### RSL-001

- Classification: FACT.
- Claim: Slavery was an integrated Roman economic and social institution affecting agriculture, households, mining, commerce, administration, and elite status; supply included warfare, piracy, birth, sale, and other mechanisms.
- Confidence: HIGH.
- Source: Springer overview; Cambridge research survey; Roman slavery and material-culture scholarship.[web:111][web:115][web:118]
- Regions/missions: All regions; especially ACT-ROME-001–007, ACT-ROME-009, ACT-ROME-012, ACT-ROME-018, ACT-ROME-020–035.
- GDD change: Represent households, workshops, mines, transport, law, debt, military capture, and manumission rather than reducing slavery to plantations or auctions.
- Professional review: REQUIRED — historian of Roman slavery.
