# Accessibility Guidelines

### ACC-001

- Classification: RECONSTRUCTION.
- Claim: Provide scalable subtitles, speaker labels, translation indicators, separate audio sliders, important sound-cue visualization, color-independent UI, remappable controls, aim/stealth assistance, reduced pursuit-dog distress, and reduced flash/fire effects.
- Confidence: HIGH as an accessibility design recommendation.
- Source: Industry accessibility frameworks and game accessibility guidance; implementation requires platform-specific QA.
- Regions/missions: All missions; especially pursuit, captivity, fire, and multilingual dialogue.
- GDD change: Add accessibility requirements to UI/UX, audio, fire, and localization specifications.
- Professional review: RECOMMENDED — accessibility specialist and disabled playtesters.
