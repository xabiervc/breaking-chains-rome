# Slavery Representation Guidelines

### SEN-001

- Classification: RECONSTRUCTION/FICTION.
- Claim: Depict enslaved people as full people with expertise, relationships, disagreement, agency, humor, faith, fear, compromise, and resistance rather than collectible victims.
- Confidence: HIGH as a narrative and sensitivity principle.
- Source: Roman Slave Community research on agency and modern historical-trauma representation practice.[web:117]
- Regions/missions: All character arcs, rescue content, and dialogue.
- GDD change: Keep Livia, Eirene, Tertia, Nicanor, Bato, and local communities as co-agents; Dama is not a universal savior.
- Professional review: REQUIRED — sensitivity readers and historians.

### SEN-002

- Classification: RECONSTRUCTION.
- Claim: Violence should have context, consequences, accessibility controls, and narrative purpose; gratuitous suffering should not be a gameplay reward.
- Confidence: HIGH as a design principle.
- Regions/missions: Combat, captivity, family separation, final fire.
- GDD change: Add warnings, content controls, non-lethal alternatives where appropriate, and consequence tracking.
- Professional review: REQUIRED — sensitivity and accessibility review.
