# Narrative-System Traceability

## Required record

Every major choice must map to: stable choice ID, scene/mission ID, player knowledge, available approaches, changed variables, affected characters or nodes, immediate feedback, delayed result, recovery/irreversible status, research classification, sensitivity status, accessibility coverage, and test ID.

## Minimum examples

| Choice | Scene | Variables | Immediate result | Delayed result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet extraction | Ostia slice | trust, supplies, exposure | fewer witnesses; kinship cost disclosed | route survives; evidence remains partial |
| Public disruption | Ostia slice | reputation, organization, exposure, retaliation | hope signal; patrol response | recruitment rises; shelter risk increases |
| Evidence release | Act III operation | information, reputation, exposure, retaliation | public pressure | witnesses may relocate; political actors react |
| Fire evacuation priority | Act IV | cohesion, supplies, nodes, grief | one route opens; another closes | survivor-led node or loss recorded |

## Consistency rule

A choice is not complete until its state change is visible in a later scene, report, environment, relationship, or available approach. Hidden changes alone do not count as authored consequence.
