# Activities and Free Roam

## Activity families

- Blacksmithing and tool repair.
- Horse and mule care.
- Hunting, fishing, and legal market sale.
- Contract reading and document copying.
- Roman road discovery.
- River and coastal navigation.
- Arena training and non-lethal sparring.
- Medicine gathering and field treatment.
- Cooking and ration preparation.
- Workshop improvement.
- Courier routes.
- Rescue logistics.
- Burial and memorial rites.
- Language lessons.
- Roman games and local competitions.

## Design constraints

Activities must be culturally and geographically grounded. They are not filler collectibles. Each activity must provide a clear gameplay benefit, a social context, and a deterministic reward table.

## Free-roam encounter rules

Encounters are selected from a fixed region/date/state pool using a stable seed. They cannot create new named characters, kill canonical characters, alter main mission order, or contradict known travel conditions.
