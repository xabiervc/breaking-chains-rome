# Historical Research

## Research standard

Use primary sources, archaeological reports, museum collections, university resources, specialist books, and peer-reviewed research. Wikipedia is a discovery aid only.

## Claim labels

- `[FACT]` — broad support.
- `[RECONSTRUCTION]` — plausible interpretation.
- `[FICTION]` — invented for the game.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` — unresolved or disputed.

## Required research subjects

- Roman slavery and manumission.
- Provincial administration.
- Roman roads, rivers, and maritime travel.
- Urban Rome and fire response.
- Thrace and Thracian identity.
- Koine Greek and Latin usage.
- Egyptian, Aramaic, Punic, Celtic, and Iberian language contexts.
- Mining, agriculture, grain, shipping, and household labor.
- Ancient evidence concerning the Great Fire of Rome.

## Source ledger format

For every source record institution/author, title, date, URL or bibliography, access date, supported claims, confidence, and disagreement.

## Historical uncertainty rule

Where evidence is disputed, represent disagreement in the codex and avoid presenting a contested interpretation as fact.
