# World Bible

## World principles

The Roman Empire is represented as a connected network of roads, ports, rivers, estates, mines, military facilities, markets, legal institutions, and household economies. Every region must offer a distinct social and gameplay identity.

## Regional identities

- Italia: political visibility and dense urban surveillance.
- Sicilia: grain production and plantation-style estates.
- Graecia: maritime exchange, education, and layered civic identities.
- Thrace: frontier militarization, mountain routes, and veterans.
- Asia Minor: commercial cities and religious plurality.
- Syria: provincial courts, caravan routes, and multilingual administration.
- Aegyptus: Nile logistics, grain, ports, and language transitions.
- Africa Proconsularis: agricultural exports and Punic/Latin contact.
- Hispania: mining, contracts, and long-distance extraction.
- Gallia Narbonensis: river corridors and western trade.

## World encounter categories

- Labor and household encounters.
- Patrol and checkpoint encounters.
- Market and contract encounters.
- Medical and family encounters.
- Religious and burial encounters.
- Maritime and river encounters.
- Military and frontier encounters.
- Information and courier encounters.

All encounter templates have fixed regional pools and deterministic selection rules.
