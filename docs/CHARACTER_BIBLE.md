# Character Bible

## Character design rule

Every major character has an independent objective, a practical competence, a contradiction, a relationship conflict, and a defined survival status.

## Relationship rules

- Dama and Livia are equals in strategic importance.
- Eirene can reject Dama's plan when civilian risk violates her fixed thresholds.
- Cassia's assistance never erases her earlier complicity.
- Bato values survival and discipline over ideology.
- Tertia controls information and cannot be reduced to a messenger.
- Vindex understands the resistance but refuses to recognize its moral legitimacy.
