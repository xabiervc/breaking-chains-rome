- Lucius Fabius Varro: escapes conviction after ACT-ROME-035.

## Prohibited contradictions

- Do not end Roman slavery.
- Do not make Dama's role in the Great Fire historical fact.
- Do not make the Great Fire optional.
- Do not move the campaign to the American South.
- Do not let ambient systems kill named characters.
- Do not replace historical language research with invented pseudo-language.

## Open questions

1. Exact linguistic reconstruction for Thracian is unavailable; all Thracian dialogue requires specialist review.
2. Egyptian voice language and period register require specialist review.
3. The extent and geometry of the historical Great Fire remain disputed; the game must represent the fictional ignition without claiming a definitive historical map.
4. Regional travel times are design abstractions and must not be presented as archaeological measurements.

## Change-control rule

Any change to immutable canon requires an explicit design decision recorded in a new dated change log. Claude must not silently modify canon.
