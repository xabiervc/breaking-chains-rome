# Art Direction

## Visual thesis

The world is materially rich but socially unequal. Marble monuments coexist with cramped housing, workshops, mines, ports, kitchens, barracks, and rural labor sites.

## Act palettes

- Act I: iron, dust, brown, smoke.
- Act II: regional variation and road-weather contrast.
- Act III: ink, ochre, bronze, courtroom shadow.
- Act IV: ember, night blue, gold, ash.

## Environmental storytelling

Show ownership through marks, ledgers, architecture, clothing quality, chains, locks, transport systems, and legal notices. Do not rely solely on exposition.

## Character visual rules

Clothing reflects legal status, work, wealth, climate, region, and access to dye or trade goods. Avoid uniform “Roman” costumes across the empire.
