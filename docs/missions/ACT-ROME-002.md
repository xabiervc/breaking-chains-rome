# ACT-ROME-002 — The Ledger Room

- Act: I
- Date: AD 54, summer
- Location: Ashgrove administrative house
- Type: Infiltration, document discovery
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Reveal that the estate belongs to a transport and ownership network and establish Dama's reading skill.

## Cast

Dama; Cassia Varro; Herennius; Silia, administrative clerk.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-001 and `knowledge.transport_seal=true`.

## Ordered objectives

1. Accept the lock-repair assignment.
2. Enter the administrative house during the midday work rotation.
3. Repair the archive lock without opening the central cabinet.
4. Copy three ledger marks from the side register.
5. Leave before Cassia returns from the courtyard.
6. Hide the marks in Dama's tool roll.

## Failure and success

Opening the central cabinet causes a non-lethal ejection and closes the optional stealth route. Being seen with the marks triggers a fixed search, after which Dama must hide them in the forge. Success sets `evidence.ashgrove_transfer_marks=true`, fixes Livia's sale date seven days later, and unlocks ACT-ROME-003.

## Determinism

Archive access is 12:10–12:20. Silia checks the corridor at 12:14. Cassia enters at 12:18.

## Rewards

Read XP 150; evidence `EVID-ITA-001`; +3 Resistance Leadership reputation.
