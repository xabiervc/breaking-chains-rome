# ACT-ROME-041 — The Last Inventory

- Act: IV
- Date: AD 64, July 17
- Location: Subura planning room and Tiber warehouses
- Type: Resource allocation, final preparation
- Classification: FICTION

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-040.

## Objectives

1. Count food, water, medicine, cloth, tools, and rope.
2. Assign supplies to the three evacuation routes.
3. Confirm all six specialists.
4. Confirm the warehouse ignition route.
5. Assign the 40 captive-rescue places.
6. Sleep before the fixed final date.

## Fixed outcome

The campaign clock advances to 18 July AD 64. The final mission becomes available and cannot be postponed indefinitely.

## Rewards

Final operation unlocked; final preparation report; deterministic fire seed locked.
