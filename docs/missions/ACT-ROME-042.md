# ACT-ROME-042 — The Great Fire

- Act: IV
- Date: 18 July AD 64, fixed
- Locations: Tiber warehouses, Subura, detention compound, Circus Maximus, Cloaca Maxima
- Type: Canonical finale, liberation, fire, confrontation, escape
- Classification: FICTION within historical framework

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-036, ACT-ROME-037, ACT-ROME-038, ACT-ROME-039, ACT-ROME-040, ACT-ROME-041 and all four Act III resolutions.

## Phase 1 — Ignition

Dama reaches the fixed warehouse at the fixed ignition time after verifying that the first evacuation route is open. He ignites the prepared archive-adjacent storage structure. If the player attempts ignition before the route check, the mission enters a fixed failure state and reloads the last preparation checkpoint.

## Phase 2 — Liberation

Dama, Livia, and Eirene open the detention compound and escort 40 captives through the assigned route. The required order is: open compound; stabilize injured people; distribute water; move children and injured adults; close the route; continue to the archive.

## Phase 3 — The Archive

Dama confronts Vindex Varro beneath the Circus Maximus. Varro argues that law, debt, hunger, and status make chains unnecessary. Dama rejects the claim and defeats him with the smithing hammer established in Act I. The archive is destroyed only after the rescue list and evidence packet have been copied.

## Phase 4 — Escape

The resistance leaves through the Cloaca Maxima and reaches the Tiber route. Fire propagation, blocked streets, water points, and civilian survival follow the fixed preparation state and fire-zone data.

## Canonical outcome

Dama survives; Livia survives; Vindex dies; 40 captives are rescued; the network archive is destroyed; the Great Fire occurs in the game's fictional interpretation; Roman slavery continues.

## Acceptance tests

- Final mission date is always 18 July AD 64.
- Vindex cannot die before this mission.
- The 40-captive rescue objective is always present.
- Named specialists survive.
- The archive is destroyed only after the rescue packet is copied.
- Reloading after completion preserves fire state, archive destruction, rescued-person ledger, and epilogue unlock.
