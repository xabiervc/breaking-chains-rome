# ACT-ROME-010 — Salt on the Tiber

- Act: II
- Date: AD 56, autumn
- Location: Ostia and the Tiber warehouses
- Type: Maritime investigation, document interception
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Introduce port logistics and identify Aulus Secundus as the Alexandria network operator.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-009; Capua safe house unlocked.

## Objectives

1. Follow the river route to Ostia.
2. Identify the salt warehouse used as a document exchange.
3. Copy the ship manifest without removing the original.
4. Follow Tertia's courier signal.
5. Escape the warehouse district during the tide change.
6. Return the copy to Rome.

## Fixed outcome

The manifest links Varro's network to Aulus Secundus. The player does not yet confront Secundus.

## Determinism

Tide state, warehouse guard rotation, and courier signal are fixed by the mission date. The manifest always contains the Alexandria route and a grain cargo cover.

## Rewards

Read XP 225; evidence `EVID-OST-001`; Ostia maritime travel unlocked.
