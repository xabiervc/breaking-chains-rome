# ACT-ROME-017 — The Army's Empty Purse

- Act: II
- Date: AD 61, spring
- Location: Thrace, frontier payroll road
- Type: Military investigation, escort, faction negotiation
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Reveal that military coercion is financed through stolen wages and introduce Bato and Drusus.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-016; Thrace route discovered.

## Objectives

1. Reach the payroll depot.
2. Find the missing pay chest record.
3. Protect Bato from a fixed arrest attempt.
4. Compare the payroll seal with the Ashgrove mark.
5. Negotiate with two unpaid veterans.
6. Escape the depot before the evening muster.

## Fixed outcome

Bato joins Dama. Drusus is identified as the military procurator diverting pay.

## Rewards

Lead XP 350; Bato joins; evidence `EVID-THR-001`; military faction state becomes watchful.
