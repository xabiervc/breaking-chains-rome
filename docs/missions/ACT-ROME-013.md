# ACT-ROME-013 — Alexandria of Many Tongues

- Act: II
- Date: AD 59, spring
- Location: Alexandria
- Type: Multilingual investigation, urban navigation
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Introduce the largest linguistic transition in the campaign and make translation a practical system rather than flavor.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-012; Ostia–Alexandria sea route discovered.

## Objectives

1. Enter the harbor using Koine Greek.
2. Hire a fixed interpreter for rural Egyptian dialogue.
3. Identify the cloth warehouse district.
4. Track the transfer token from Ashgrove.
5. Locate the household where Livia was sold.
6. Leave before the harbor patrol changes shift.

## Fixed outcome

Dama confirms that Livia is alive and has moved from captive labor into a household healing network. The player does not reunite with her yet.

## Determinism

The interpreter is Nicanor's contact, Ptolemy; the transfer token is in warehouse 7; the harbor shift changes at 18:00.

## Rewards

Read XP 300; Koine Greek level 2; Egyptian comprehension level 1; Livia survival confirmed.
