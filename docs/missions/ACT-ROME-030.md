- Target: Marcus Laelius Drusus (`CHAR-007`)

- Location: Thracian frontier fort (`LOC-THR-001`)
- Type: Sabotage, military infiltration

ACT-ROME-029.

1. Enter as a repair worker.
2. Locate the payroll standard.

4. Open the pay-store route.
5. Escape before inspection.
6. Deliver the standard to Bato.
