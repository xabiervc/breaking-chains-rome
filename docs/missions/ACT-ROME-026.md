# ACT-ROME-026 — Letters to Rome

- Act: III
- Target: Gaius Valerius Crispus (`CHAR-006`)
- Date: AD 63, autumn
- Location: Antioch archive and courier road (`LOC-SYR-001`)
- Type: Evidence copying, courier network
- Classification: FICTION

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-025.

## Objectives

1. Enter the archive through the fixed service entrance.
2. Locate letters bearing the senatorial seal.
3. Copy payment and appointment references.
4. Send verified copies through three courier routes.
5. Hide the original correspondence from the archive search.

## Fixed outcome

`EVID-SYR-004` is verified as public-leverage evidence. The letters establish that Crispus's decisions were protected in Rome.
