# ACT-ROME-040 — The Archive Beneath the Circus

- Act: IV
- Date: AD 64, June
- Location: Circus Maximus archive complex
- Type: Archive infiltration, evidence prioritization, rescue planning
- Classification: FICTION

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-039; final archive route discovered in ACT-ROME-034.

## Objectives

1. Enter the archive through the fixed service passage.
2. Catalogue the ledgers by origin and destination.
3. Mark the records needed to identify captives and safe routes.
4. Copy the final evidence packet.
5. Open the detention-compound map.
6. Leave the archive intact.

## Fixed outcome

The player obtains the final archive map and prioritization list. The archive cannot be destroyed before the final mission.

## Rewards

Archive map; final rescue list; evidence packet; fire-operation route.
