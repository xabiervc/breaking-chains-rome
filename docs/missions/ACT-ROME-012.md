# ACT-ROME-012 — Island of Debts

- Act: II
- Date: AD 58, summer
- Location: Syracuse and inland Sicily
- Type: Economic sabotage, contract investigation
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Connect grain production, debt contracts, estate labor, and maritime power.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-011; Sicily route introduced by Tertia.

## Objectives

1. Reach Syracuse by merchant ship.
2. Identify the estate's debt registrar.
3. Copy the contract chain linking grain and forced labor.
4. Replace the next shipment's debt markers.
5. Deliver food to the inland work camp.
6. Escape the estate before the audit.

## Fixed outcome

The estate loses one season of records; the resistance gains a grain contact. No mass uprising occurs in this mission.

## Rewards

Read XP 250; evidence `EVID-SIC-001`; grain-supply modifier -10% for Act II travel costs.
