# ACT-ROME-011 — The Grain Road

- Act: II
- Date: AD 57, spring
- Location: Campania grain road
- Type: Escort, stealth, logistics
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Demonstrate that an escape network needs food, routes, and local trust. Introduce the rescue ledger at operational scale.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-010.

## Objectives

1. Collect the grain allotment from the workshop.
2. Escort six fugitives through the Campanian road network.
3. Avoid the fixed checkpoint inspection.
4. Split the group at the documented rest location.
5. Deliver food to the Capua safe house.
6. Return the empty carts without exposing the route.

## Fixed outcome

All six fugitives reach assigned destinations. The safe-house capacity increases by 6.

## Rewards

Lead XP 250; safe-house capacity +6; food-supply route unlocked.
