# ACT-ROME-007 — The Overseer's Road

- Act: I
- Date: AD 55, spring
- Locations: Alban Hills Refuge (`LOC-ITA-005`), Ashgrove perimeter (`LOC-ITA-001`), register house, Appian route
- Type: Return infiltration, revenge, irreversible transition
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Complete Act I. Dama retrieves evidence and confronts the person representing his personal history of violence.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-006 and `evidence.ashgrove_transfer_marks=true`.

## Ordered objectives

1. Depart Alban Hills Refuge.
2. Return to the Ashgrove perimeter.
3. Enter the old register house.
4. Recover the copied ledger and burn the local register.
5. Avoid the fixed patrol route.
6. Confront Herennius in the forge yard.
7. Defeat Herennius with the smithing hammer.
8. Escape toward Alban Hills Refuge.

## Fixed outcome

Herennius dies; the empire-wide fugitive state activates; Act II becomes available; Dama does not return to Ashgrove during the canonical campaign.
