# ACT-ROME-008 — A City of Masks

- Act: II
- Date: AD 56, spring
- Location: Rome
- Type: Urban infiltration, identity management
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Move Dama from rural flight into imperial urban surveillance. Introduce forged identities, dense crowds, urban districts, and Tertia.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-007.

## Objectives

1. Enter Rome through the Porta Capena route.
2. Exchange the Ashgrove travel token for a temporary labor identity.
3. Cross the Forum without attracting a lockdown.
4. Locate Tertia in the Subura.
5. Deliver the copied marks and Herennius's register fragment.
6. Reach the safe room before the third watch.

## Fixed outcome

Tertia accepts Dama as a provisional member of the resistance network. Dama's Latin administrative vocabulary increases to level 2.

## Determinism

The gate inspection occurs at 09:20; Tertia's courier handoff occurs at 16:10; the safe room remains available at `LOC-ITA-003.SUBURA.SR-01`.

## Rewards

Read XP 200; Latin level 2; Rome free-roam unlock; Tertia relationship +1.
