# ACT-ROME-016 — The Governor's Shadow

- Act: II
- Date: AD 60, spring
- Location: Antioch
- Type: Political investigation, disguise, document trail
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Move the conspiracy from commerce to provincial government and introduce Gaius Valerius Crispus.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-015; Syria route discovered.

## Objectives

1. Enter Antioch under a merchant identity.
2. Locate the provincial debt court.
3. Identify the clerk receiving Varro payments.
4. Copy two court orders.
5. Follow the caravan account trail.
6. Escape the court archive through the service court.

## Fixed outcome

Crispus is identified as the provincial protector of the network. Dama receives the first court evidence but cannot expose him yet.

## Rewards

Read XP 325; Aramaic comprehension level 1; evidence `EVID-SYR-001`.
