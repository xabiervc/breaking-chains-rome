# ACT-ROME-038 — Six Hands

- Act: IV
- Date: AD 64, March–April
- Location: Rome, Subura workshop and safe houses
- Type: Recruitment, relationship, preparation
- Classification: FICTION

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-037; Livia, Nicanor, Tertia, Bato, Eirene, and Publius available.

## Objectives

1. Hold the six-person planning council.
2. Assign one fixed responsibility to each specialist.
3. Resolve Livia and Dama's disagreement about civilian risk.
4. Resolve Bato and Eirene's disagreement about defensive force.
5. Create the first complete evacuation timetable.
6. Store the timetable in two safe locations.

## Fixed outcome

All six specialists accept their responsibilities. Tier 2 preparation is complete; the player cannot remove a specialist from the final operation.

## Rewards

Specialist command interface; planning-room upgrade; relationship entries.
