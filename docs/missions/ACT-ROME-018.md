# ACT-ROME-018 — The Mine Road

- Act: II
- Date: AD 62, summer
- Location: Corduba and Hispania Baetica mining district
- Type: Mine infiltration, labor rescue, contract destruction
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Show the financial extraction behind the network and add a non-maritime western branch.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-017; Hispania route discovered.

## Objectives

1. Enter the mine as a tool inspector.
2. Locate the silver contract archive.
3. Copy the ownership and shipment clauses.
4. Free the injured maintenance workers.
5. Destroy the duplicate contract plates.
6. Reach Corduba before the mine supervisor reports the breach.

## Fixed outcome

The mining contract is destroyed; the workers reach a fixed refuge; the evidence identifies Lucius Fabius Varro as the legal protector in Rome.

## Rewards

Forge XP 300; Read XP 300; evidence `EVID-HIS-001`; mine refuge unlocked.
