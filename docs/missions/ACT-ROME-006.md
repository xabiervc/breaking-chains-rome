# ACT-ROME-006 — Ash in the Hills

- Act: I
- Date: AD 55, winter, two days after escape
- Location: Alban Hills refuge
- Type: Survival, resource allocation, leadership
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Show that escape is not safety. Introduce food, medicine, fatigue, and group responsibility.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-005.

## Ordered objectives

1. Establish shelter.
2. Count food and medicine.
3. Treat Vara's wound.
4. Assign a night watch.
5. Find water before sunrise.
6. Decide whether to remain hidden or retrieve the copied ledger.
7. Survive the fixed night patrol.

## Fixed outcome

Vara dies from an internal injury. The memorial entry and reason to retrieve the ledger are always created.

## Rewards and tests

Lead XP 150; Alban Hills refuge unlocked; resource ledger unlocked; +5 Resistance Leadership. Vara's death is scripted and the resource ledger persists.
