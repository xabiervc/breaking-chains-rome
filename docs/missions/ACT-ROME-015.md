# ACT-ROME-015 — The Harbor Without Chains

- Act: II
- Date: AD 59, autumn
- Location: Alexandria harbor warehouses
- Type: Coordinated rescue, engineering, stealth
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Teach that freeing people is inseparable from transport, food, shelter, and timing.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-014; Nicanor contact unlocked.

## Objectives

1. Map the warehouse water channels.
2. Prepare two escape routes.
3. Disable the lock system without igniting the harbor.
4. Open the holding room.
5. Move the captives to the river craft.
6. Deliver the ledger copy to Livia.
7. Leave before the harbor watch closes the quay.

## Fixed outcome

A fixed group of 18 captives reaches safe accommodation. Livia remains in Alexandria; she does not leave with Dama.

## Rewards

Lead XP 350; Nicanor joins; 18 rescue-ledger entries; warehouse route unlocked.
