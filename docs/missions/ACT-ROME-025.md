# ACT-ROME-025 — The Caravan Witness

- Act: III
- Target: Gaius Valerius Crispus (`CHAR-006`)
- Date: AD 63, summer
- Location: Antioch–Damascus caravan route (`LOC-SYR-001`)
- Type: Escort, witness protection, desert logistics
- Classification: FICTION

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-024.

## Objectives

1. Prepare water and shade supplies.
2. Locate the caravan witness.
3. Escort the witness through the fixed route.
4. Protect the witness from the scheduled interception.
5. Reach the protected caravan station.
6. Record the testimony.

## Fixed outcome

`EVID-SYR-003` is verified as personal-link evidence. The witness survives and becomes available for ACT-ROME-027.
