# ACT-ROME-019 — The Four Seals

- Act: II
- Date: AD 62, winter
- Location: Massilia, Gallia Narbonensis
- Type: Evidence synthesis, travel planning, act transition
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Convert separate regional investigations into a coherent conspiracy and unlock the four Act III target chains.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-018; at least one of the following evidence objects: `EVID-OST-001`, `EVID-AEG-001`, `EVID-SYR-001`, `EVID-THR-001`, or `EVID-HIS-001`. Main progression guarantees the required minimum set.

## Objectives

1. Reach Massilia using the western route.
2. Meet Tertia's merchant contact.
3. Arrange the five evidence objects on the wax map.
4. Identify the four power centers.
5. Choose the first target route.
6. Send fixed messages to the three remaining regions.

## Fixed outcome

Act III unlocks. The four target chains become available in any order, but all four resolution missions are required before Act IV.

## Rewards

Read XP 500; Lead XP 300; four target dossiers; Act III free travel between known regions.
