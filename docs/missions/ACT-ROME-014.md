# ACT-ROME-014 — The Textile Cipher

- Act: II
- Date: AD 59, summer
- Location: Alexandria textile quarter
- Type: Investigation, relationship, stealth
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Reveal Livia's agency and transform the rescue plot into a cooperative resistance plot.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-013.

## Objectives

1. Decode the cloth marker.
2. Follow the marked textile route.
3. Avoid exposing the household healer network.
4. Meet Livia in the bathhouse service corridor.
5. Exchange the Ashgrove token.
6. Leave separately through two fixed exits.

## Fixed outcome

Dama and Livia meet briefly. Livia refuses immediate extraction because her patients and contacts would be exposed. She gives Dama the name Aulus Secundus and a ledger-room symbol.

## Rewards

Read XP 275; Livia relationship +3; evidence `EVID-AEG-001`; healer network unlocked.
