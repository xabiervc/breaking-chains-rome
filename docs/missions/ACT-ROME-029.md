# ACT-ROME-029 — The Veteran's Oath

- Act: III
- Target: Marcus Laelius Drusus (`CHAR-007`)
- Date: AD 63, summer
- Location: Thracian veteran settlement (`LOC-THR-001`)
- Type: Alliance, negotiation, escort
- Classification: FICTION

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-028.

## Objectives

1. Reach the veteran settlement.
2. Present the copied payroll totals.
3. Protect two unpaid veterans from arrest.
4. Negotiate a non-execution oath.
5. Escort the veterans to the mountain refuge.
6. Record their testimony.

## Fixed outcome

`EVID-THR-003` is verified as personal-link evidence. The veterans join mutiny preparation and no civilian massacre occurs.
