# ACT-ROME-037 — Watchmen of the Night

- Act: IV
- Date: AD 64, February
- Location: Rome fire-watch posts
- Type: Surveillance, social stealth, recruitment
- Classification: FICTION within historical framework

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-036.

## Objectives

1. Observe three fixed watch rotations.
2. Identify officers who respond to civilian calls.
3. Prevent a false alarm from reaching the Subura.
4. Contact Publius Caecilius.
5. Recover the watch route tablet.
6. Escort Publius to Eirene's planning room.

## Fixed outcome

Publius joins the operation. Fire-watch routes become visible. Tier 2 evacuation becomes available after supplies are collected.

## Rewards

Publius recruited; watch-route data; +1 civilian warning capacity.
