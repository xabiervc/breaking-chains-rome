# ACT-ROME-027 — The Governor Falls

- Act: III
- Target: Gaius Valerius Crispus (`CHAR-006`)
- Date: AD 63, winter
- Location: Antioch civic quarter (`LOC-SYR-001`)
- Type: Target resolution, political exposure
- Classification: FICTION

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-024, ACT-ROME-025, and ACT-ROME-026.

## Objectives

1. Publish the verified letters through three channels.
2. Protect the witness during the public hearing.
3. Prevent the court from seizing the copies.
4. Reach the resistance safe house.
5. Send the final dossier to Rome.

## Fixed outcome

Crispus is recalled to Rome. The provincial court becomes restricted, caravan witnesses gain protection, and `target.antioch.resolved=true`.
