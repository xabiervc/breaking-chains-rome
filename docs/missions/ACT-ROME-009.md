# ACT-ROME-009 — The Ludus Below

- Act: II
- Date: AD 56, summer
- Location: Capua gladiatorial school
- Type: Infiltration, rescue, close-quarters escape
- Classification: FICTION

## Purpose

Show another branch of the trafficking economy and establish that liberation requires a destination and supplies.

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-008; Rome–Capua route discovered.

## Objectives

1. Travel to Capua under a forged labor identity.
2. Enter the ludus as a tool repairer.
3. Find the missing courier's wax tablet.
4. Open the lower holding room.
5. Lead two captives through the maintenance passage.
6. Recover the courier's message.
7. Reach the fixed safe house.

## Fixed outcome

Two captives reach the safe house; one remains in Capua as an informant by choice. The wax tablet identifies an Ostia manifest.

## Determinism

The lower passage opens at 22:15 after the water inspection. The two rescued captives are always Sura and Menon.

## Rewards

Move XP 150; evidence `EVID-CAP-001`; Capua safe house unlocked.
