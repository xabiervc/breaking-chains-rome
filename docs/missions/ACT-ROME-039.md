# ACT-ROME-039 — The Evacuation Houses

- Act: IV
- Date: AD 64, May
- Location: Subura, Aventine, Ostia route
- Type: Shelter preparation, supply logistics, civilian protection
- Classification: FICTION

## Prerequisites

ACT-ROME-038.

## Objectives

1. Secure the underground storage complex near the Subura.
2. Prepare the Aventine garden refuge.
3. Clear the warehouse route toward Ostia.
4. Deliver fixed quantities of food, water, cloth, and medicine.
5. Assign civilian groups to shelters.
6. Confirm the three evacuation routes with Eirene.

## Fixed outcome

Tier 3 evacuation becomes available if the player has completed the required supply side missions. Without them, Tier 2 remains the valid state; the mission itself always succeeds.

## Rewards

Three evacuation routes; shelter capacity; food and medical ledgers updated.
