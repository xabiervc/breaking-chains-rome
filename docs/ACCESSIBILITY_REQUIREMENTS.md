# Accessibility Requirements

## Measurable requirements

- Text supports a user-selectable minimum effective body size of 18 px at 1080p, with scaling to at least 200%; no critical text may fall below the selected minimum.
- UI text and essential icons meet WCAG AA contrast targets where applicable, with tested high-contrast, deuteranopia, protanopia, and tritanopia-friendly modes; color is never the sole carrier of meaning.
- Subtitles support size, background opacity, speaker labels, sound-effect captions, direction, timing, language/translation state, and safe-area placement.
- Every essential audio cue has a configurable visual analogue; every essential visual state has text or audio redundancy where practical.
- All actions are remappable, including hold/toggle alternatives, repeated-input alternatives, and one-button accessibility shortcuts.
- Quick-time and rapid button sequences have hold, toggle, slow-motion, or auto-complete alternatives.
- Gameplay speed is adjustable for combat, stealth detection, chase pressure, and fire evacuation where state logic permits.
- Difficulty is separated into combat, stealth, resource pressure, puzzle assistance, navigation, and narrative consequence visibility.
- Checkpoints occur before high-stress sequences and after major irreversible decisions; retrying does not erase accessibility settings.
- Keyboard/mouse, controller, and supported assistive input paths receive parity testing.
- Test matrix includes blind/low-vision, deaf/hard-of-hearing, motor, cognitive, and neurodivergent players through specialist review and compensated playtests.

## Evidence

Every requirement needs a test case, platform result, defect disposition, and playtest note before the release-candidate gate.
