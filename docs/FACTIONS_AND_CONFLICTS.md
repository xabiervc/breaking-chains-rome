# Factions and Conflicts

## Major factions

| ID | Faction | Goal | Public face | Hidden resource |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FACTION-001 | Varro Network | Profit from human ownership and labor | Legal commerce | Ledgers and transport contracts |
| FACTION-002 | Imperial Administration | Preserve order and tax flow | Law and governance | Courts, patrols, records |
| FACTION-003 | Free Communities | Protect families and livelihoods | Neighborhood, workshop, church-like associations | Safe houses and local knowledge |
| FACTION-004 | Resistance Network | Liberate people and destroy infrastructure | Mutual aid | Couriers, workshops, evidence |
| FACTION-005 | Military Establishment | Maintain frontier and internal security | Army discipline | Troops, roads, coercion |
| FACTION-006 | Rival Merchants | Capture market share | Trade competition | Ships, credit, political access |

## Conflict axes

1. Ownership versus personhood.
2. Order versus justice.
3. Personal revenge versus collective safety.
4. Information control versus testimony.
5. Imperial stability versus local survival.

## Faction state rules

Each faction has a deterministic state from 0 to 4: dormant, watchful, active, aggressive, collapsing. Mission completions change states through fixed transition tables documented in the systems documents. No faction state changes from untracked background randomness.
