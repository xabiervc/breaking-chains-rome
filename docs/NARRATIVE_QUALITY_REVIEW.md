# Narrative Quality Review

## Strengths to preserve

- Dama's arc moves from personal survival toward collective agency.
- The Great Fire is a moral and systemic climax, not only an action set piece.
- Livia, Eirene, Tertia, Nicanor, Bato, and local communities must retain independent objectives.
- Historical uncertainty is used honestly where the record is incomplete.

## Required improvements

- Give every Act a governing question, reversal, midpoint cost, and irreversible consequence.
- Tie each main mission to at least one character want and one systemic pressure.
- Ensure the player learns why a choice matters before being asked to make it.
- Make epilogue outcomes reflect accumulated relationships, not only a binary success variable.
- Avoid making Dama morally flawless or uniquely capable of liberation.
- Separate historical record, reconstruction, and fiction in codex language.

## Narrative QA tests

- A new player can summarize Dama's immediate goal after the first playable mission.
- Each Act has a visible short-term objective and an implicit thematic question.
- No companion disappears from the story without an authored explanation.
- Choice feedback is legible without reducing complex history to a morality meter.
- Trauma content has framing, warnings, player controls, and consequence-aware presentation.
