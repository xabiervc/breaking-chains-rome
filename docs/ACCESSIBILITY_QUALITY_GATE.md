# Accessibility Quality Gate

## Required before release candidate

- Scalable text and subtitles with safe-area validation.
- Speaker labels, translation state, and non-color identifiers.
- Full input remapping, alternate hold/toggle modes, and controller/keyboard parity where supported.
- Separate volume controls for dialogue, music, ambience, combat, and distressing effects.
- Visual indicators for essential audio cues with timing and intensity controls.
- Reduced flash, camera shake, fire intensity, and pursuit-dog distress options.
- Difficulty assists for stealth detection, chase pressure, combat timing, puzzle hints, and resource scarcity.
- Save-anywhere or generous checkpoint policy around high-stress sequences.
- No essential information conveyed only by color, audio, rapid flashes, or tiny text.
- Captions for dialogue, non-speech sounds, direction, speaker, and important environmental changes.

## Evidence

Each feature needs a test case, platform result, and playtest note. Accessibility is a design constraint from the first greybox, not a final settings menu.

## Professional review

Recommended: accessibility specialist plus disabled players representing relevant access needs. Their feedback is a production gate, not optional marketing input.
