# Canonical Chronology Decision — Act I

## Decision

Act I takes place over one calendar year, from AD 54 spring through AD 55 spring. The interval between Livia's sale and Dama's escape is intentional and canonical.

## Fixed schedule

| Mission | Canonical date | Narrative meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ACT-ROME-001 | AD 54, spring | Dama learns the estate schedule and observes the transport seal. |
| ACT-ROME-002 | AD 54, summer | Dama copies the transfer marks. |
| ACT-ROME-003 | AD 54, late summer, seven days before sale | Dama and Livia create the textile cipher. |
| ACT-ROME-004 | AD 54, late summer, sale day | Livia is transferred toward Alexandria. |
| Interstitial period | AD 54 autumn–AD 55 winter | Dama remains under surveillance, gathers tools, maintains the cipher, and waits for a viable escape opportunity. These events are represented by authored ambient scenes and optional side content, not additional main missions. |
| ACT-ROME-005 | AD 55, winter | Dama sabotages the transport wagon and escapes with Nera, Silvanus, and Otho. |
| ACT-ROME-006 | AD 55, winter, two days after escape | The fugitives reach the Alban Hills refuge; Vara dies. |
| ACT-ROME-007 | AD 55, spring | Dama returns to the Ashgrove perimeter, retrieves the register, kills Herennius, and destroys the local register. |

## Why the gap exists

The gap prevents the escape from feeling like an immediate reaction that would make the plantation's surveillance implausibly weak. It also gives Livia's coded communications and Dama's preparation narrative weight.

## Player-facing presentation

At the end of ACT-ROME-004, show a deterministic time passage montage: seasonal changes, repeated work shifts, coded textile fragments, failed escape preparations, and increased patrols. The player resumes direct control at the beginning of AD 55 winter.

## Canonical constraints

- Dama cannot escape before ACT-ROME-005.
- Livia's transfer cannot be prevented in ACT-ROME-004.
- The time passage cannot be skipped as if no events occurred; it must be represented through authored scenes and optional interactions.
- No new main mission may be inserted into the gap without updating the canon manifest and dependency graph.
