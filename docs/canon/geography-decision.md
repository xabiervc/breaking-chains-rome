# Canonical Geography Decision — Act I

## Decision

Alban Hills is a distinct rural refuge location south-east of Rome and must not reuse the Rome metropolis ID.

## Canonical location

- **ID:** `LOC-ITA-005`.
- **Name:** Alban Hills Refuge.
- **Region:** Italia.
- **Type:** rural_refuge.
- **Languages:** Latin, regional Italian varieties, limited Thracian.
- **Security:** 1 before pursuit escalation; 2 after ACT-ROME-005.
- **Classification:** FACT/RECONSTRUCTION for the geographic area; FICTION for the refuge and events.

## Location relationships

- Ashgrove Estate: `LOC-ITA-001`.
- Capua: `LOC-ITA-002`.
- Rome: `LOC-ITA-003`.
- Ostia: `LOC-ITA-004`.
- Alban Hills Refuge: `LOC-ITA-005`.

## Canonical use

- ACT-ROME-005 ends at the olive-grove hiding point en route to the refuge.
- ACT-ROME-006 takes place at `LOC-ITA-005`.
- ACT-ROME-007 begins at `LOC-ITA-005` and returns to the Ashgrove perimeter.
- The refuge remains available during Act I and becomes a low-security historical location after ACT-ROME-007.
