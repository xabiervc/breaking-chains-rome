# Canonical Final-State Decision

## Mission gate

ACT-ROME-042 is available only after ACT-ROME-041. ACT-ROME-041 is available only after ACT-ROME-040, and ACT-ROME-040 requires all four Act III resolution missions through its archive-route state.

## Evacuation

EVAC-001 is mandatory and has capacity 40 at Tier 0. EVAC-002 and EVAC-003 are optional capacity routes. The final mission is completable at all four tiers.

## Fire

The canonical sequence begins only after `final_mission_started=true` and `evacuation.route_1_open=true`. Optional fire zones may remain unlit; they cannot prevent the archive zone from burning after the rescue packet is copied.

## Ending invariants

- Dama survives unidentified.
- Livia and all six specialists survive.
- Vindex dies in ACT-ROME-042.
- Exactly 40 captives are mandatory rescues.
- The network archive is destroyed after the rescue packet is copied.
- Roman slavery continues.
