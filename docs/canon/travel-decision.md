# Canonical Travel and Act III Order Decision

## Travel authority

`data/route-definitions.yaml` is the canonical route graph. A route's `unlock_mission` must be completed before the route becomes available.

## Act II travel unlocks

- Rome–Capua: ACT-ROME-008.
- Rome–Ostia: ACT-ROME-009.
- Ostia–Alexandria: ACT-ROME-010.
- Alexandria–Antioch: ACT-ROME-015.
- Antioch–Thrace: ACT-ROME-016.
- Thrace–Hispania: ACT-ROME-017.
- Hispania–Massilia: ACT-ROME-018.

## Act III order

The player may choose any of the four target arcs after ACT-ROME-019. Calendar time is not advanced by choosing a different target; each arc's date window is an authored historical-fiction presentation window, and the campaign clock resolves the completed arc at the fixed AD 63 seasonal milestone assigned to that mission. This avoids impossible simultaneous travel while preserving player choice.

The final Act III resolution missions are mandatory: ACT-ROME-023, ACT-ROME-027, ACT-ROME-031, and ACT-ROME-035.
