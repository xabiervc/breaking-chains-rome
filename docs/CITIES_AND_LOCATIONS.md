## Rome district catalogue

- Subura: dense housing, workshops, couriers, civilian evacuation.
- Forum: legal and political visibility.
- Circus Maximus: archive, crowds, final confrontation access.
- Aventine: gardens, refuge, elite and working-class overlap.
- Esquiline: warehouses, tenements, fire risk.
- Tiber warehouses: trade, records, ignition route.
- Cloaca Maxima: final escape route.

Every location document must define entrances, exits, vertical layers, residents, factions, schedules, resources, missions, and historical classification.
