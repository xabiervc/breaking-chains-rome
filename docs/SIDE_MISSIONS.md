# Side Missions

## Side-mission design

Side missions expand agency, history, relationships, resources, language, and civilian survival. They never contradict canonical main missions or remove the fixed climax.

## Act I side missions

- SIDE-ITA-001 The Missing Bell — stealth alert tutorial.
- SIDE-ITA-002 Nera's Herbs — field treatment.
- SIDE-ITA-003 Silvanus's Debt — companion morale.
- SIDE-ITA-004 The Smith's Apprentices — workshop unlock.
- SIDE-ITA-005 The Names on the Wall — memorial and codex.

## Fixed side-mission effects

Each side mission has one primary reward, one relationship effect, and one world-state effect. A side mission cannot alter the identities of main targets, the final date, or the canonical outcome.
