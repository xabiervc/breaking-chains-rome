# Historical Sensitivity

- Do not romanticize slavery.
- Do not reduce enslaved people to victims waiting for rescue.
- Give enslaved and freed characters agency, expertise, family, faith, resistance, compromise, and conflicting strategies.
- Do not project modern racial categories uncritically onto the Roman world.
- Distinguish household slavery, war captivity, debt bondage, penal labor, mining labor, agricultural labor, and manumission.
- Do not make Dama the savior of everyone; Livia, Eirene, Tertia, Nicanor, Bato, and local communities have independent agency.
- Use content warnings and accessibility options for scenes involving violence, coercion, family separation, and captivity.
- Consult historians and sensitivity readers before production lock.
