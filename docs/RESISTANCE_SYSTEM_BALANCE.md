# Resistance System Balance

## Principles

- No variable is always good or bad.
- Every major action changes at least two variables in opposite directions.
- A visible benefit carries a visible or discoverable risk.
- Recovery is possible but never free.
- Quiet play, collective play, negotiation, sabotage, and escape are all viable.
- The player cannot optimize without listening to people inside the network.

## Bounded model

Use 0–100 for Trust, Supplies, Exposure, Reputation, Organization, Retaliation, Information, and Cohesion. Exposure and Retaliation are pressure variables; high values are dangerous. Do not collapse them into one score.

```text
public_disruption:
  hope_signal: +12
  reputation: +10
  organization: +5
  exposure: +18
  retaliation: +15
  supplies: -8
  trust: -2..+8 depending on witnesses
```

Exact values must be playtested, not treated as historical measurements.

## Anti-exploit tests

- Repeating one action cannot dominate all strategies.
- Killing guards cannot be cheapest in every mission.
- Hoarding supplies cannot outperform timely distribution in every Act.
- Maximum reputation cannot guarantee cooperation.
- High organization cannot erase grief, disagreement, or betrayal.
- Failed rescue cannot be reset away as the only meaningful response; the network must adapt.
