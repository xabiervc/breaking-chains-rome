# Award-Caliber Quality Bar

## Purpose

This document translates an aspirational award-level target into production criteria. It does not claim nomination, victory, certification, or external jury approval.

The target is a coherent, distinctive, emotionally consequential, accessible, technically reliable game whose strongest qualities reinforce one another.

## Five judging lenses

1. Creative identity: a memorable premise, visual language, sound identity, and authorial point of view.
2. Player experience: mechanics that express the themes rather than merely decorating them.
3. Narrative craft: clear character wants, meaningful reversals, consequence, and earned emotional payoff.
4. Inclusion and care: accessibility, historical sensitivity, respectful representation, and transparent content controls.
5. Craft and reliability: stable builds, readable UX, deterministic data, performance budgets, and polished onboarding.

## Non-negotiable gates

- No critical or blocker defects in the release candidate.
- Every main mission has an authored objective, escalation, choice/consequence beat, accessibility coverage, and regression case.
- Every historical claim is classified and sourced; uncertain reconstructions are not presented as fact.
- Every ancient-language line has provenance, intended register, translation, and specialist-review status.
- Every core mechanic passes a teach-use-master loop in the vertical slice.
- Subtitles, remapping, visual sound cues, reduced-flash options, and difficulty assists are present before content lock.
- Performance and save/load budgets are measured on target hardware, not assumed.
