# Region — Italia

## Identity

Italia is the political and logistical heart of the campaign. It combines dense urban surveillance, estates, roads, ports, forums, workshops, military administration, and the final city disaster.

## Fixed hubs

Rome, Capua, Ostia, Pompeii, Brundisium, Ravenna, Alban Hills, Appian Way.

## Gameplay identity

- Urban identity and checkpoint management.
- Estate infiltration and document theft.
- River and coastal travel.
- Political exposure.
- Final evacuation and fire operations.

## Languages

Primary: Classical Latin. Secondary: Koine Greek and regional varieties.

## Factions

Imperial Administration, Varro Network, Free Communities, Resistance Network, Rival Merchants.

## Security

Rome and Ostia use security level 4; Capua and rural routes use level 3; the Alban Hills use level 1 until a pursuit state is active.

## Historical classification

Major cities and roads are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION; Ashgrove Estate and its characters are FICTION; the gameplay abstraction is FICTIONALIZED SCALE.
