# Region — Thrace

## Identity

Thrace is Dama's ancestral region and a militarized frontier space. It explores identity, veterans, recruitment, mountain travel, and the tension between local memory and imperial service.

## Fixed hubs

Byzantium, Philippopolis, frontier forts, mountain villages, military payroll depots.

## Gameplay identity

Military patrols, escort missions, mountain routes, mutiny preparation, veteran networks, and heritage conversations.

## Languages

Primary: regional Thracian reconstruction and Greek. Administrative: Latin and Greek. Thracian dialogue requires specialist review.

## Historical classification

Region, cities, and military presence are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION; Dama's family and resistance events are FICTION.
