# Region — Africa Proconsularis

## Identity

North Africa is an optional but substantial agricultural and maritime region. It provides rival merchants, export logistics, Punic/Latin contact, and an alternative western route to Rome.

## Fixed hubs

Carthage, Utica, coastal farms, merchant ports.

## Gameplay identity

Export contracts, coastal travel, merchant rivalry, agriculture, and safe-house diplomacy.

## Languages

Primary: Latin and Punic. Secondary: Greek in commercial settings. Punic voice work requires specialist review.

## Historical classification

Cities and trade are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION; all resistance plots are FICTION.
