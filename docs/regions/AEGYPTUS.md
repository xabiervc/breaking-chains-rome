# Region — Aegyptus

## Identity

Egypt is the grain, Nile, port, textile, and language-transition region. Alexandria is the largest eastern hub and the place where Dama reunites with Livia.

## Fixed hubs

Alexandria, Memphis, Nile settlements, quarry routes, harbor warehouses.

## Gameplay identity

Harbor logistics, river travel, textile codes, translators, healer networks, warehouses, and grain administration.

## Languages

Alexandria: Koine Greek, Latin, Egyptian. Rural Egypt: Egyptian and Greek, with Latin in official contexts. Egyptian voice work requires specialist review.

## Historical classification

Alexandria and Nile logistics are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION; Livia's network and Aulus Secundus are FICTION.
