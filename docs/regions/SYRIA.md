# Region — Syria

## Identity

Syria is the administrative and caravan region. It places Dama against courts, tax systems, military escorts, desert logistics, and multilingual provincial power.

## Fixed hubs

Antioch, Damascus, caravan stations, eastern military roads.

## Gameplay identity

Court infiltration, caravan escort, desert preparation, evidence protection, and political exposure.

## Languages

Primary: Western Aramaic and Koine Greek. Administrative: Latin. Regional multilingualism is mandatory in dialogue design.

## Historical classification

Cities, provincial administration, and language contact are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION; Crispus and the plot are FICTION.
