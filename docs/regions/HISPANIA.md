# Region — Hispania

## Identity

Hispania Baetica is the extraction and mining region. It links silver, contracts, labor, tools, and distant finance to the imperial network.

## Fixed hubs

Tarraco, Corduba, Gades, mining districts, river routes.

## Gameplay identity

Mine infiltration, tool repair, contract copying, labor rescue, and long-distance western travel.

## Languages

Primary: Latin. Secondary: regional Iberian languages where research supports inclusion.

## Historical classification

Mining and cities are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION; the fictional contract network and missions are FICTION.
