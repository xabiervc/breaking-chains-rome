# Region — Graecia

## Identity

Graecia is the education, maritime, and civic-identity region. It provides optional language training, merchant contacts, philosophical debates, and routes between the western and eastern empire.

## Fixed hubs

Athens, Corinth, Thessalonica, Philippi, Aegean ports.

## Gameplay identity

Language lessons, ship contracts, debates, courier routes, temples, workshops, and coastal travel.

## Languages

Primary: Koine Greek. Secondary: Latin and regional Greek varieties.
