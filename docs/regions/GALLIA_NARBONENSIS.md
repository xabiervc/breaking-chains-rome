# Region — Gallia Narbonensis

## Identity

Gallia Narbonensis is the western river and merchant crossroads. Massilia serves as the evidence-synthesis location before Act III.

## Fixed hubs

Massilia, Nemausus, Rhône routes, coastal merchant stations.

## Gameplay identity

River travel, merchant diplomacy, western courier routes, evidence synthesis, and optional Celtic-language encounters.

## Languages

Primary: Latin and Greek in trade. Secondary: regional Celtic languages. Celtic voice work requires specialist review.

## Historical classification

Cities, Roman administration, and trade routes are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION; all central plot events are FICTION.
