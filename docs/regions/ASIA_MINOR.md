# Region — Asia Minor

## Identity

Asia Minor connects coastal cities, inland roads, religious communities, merchant houses, and eastern imperial administration.

## Fixed hubs

Ephesus, Smyrna, Tarsus, coastal roads, inland courier stations.

## Gameplay identity

Commercial investigation, letter networks, multilingual negotiation, route planning, and optional evidence missions.

## Languages

Primary: Koine Greek and Latin. Secondary: regional Anatolian languages where research supports inclusion.

## Historical classification

Major cities and trade routes are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION; game locations and characters are FICTION.
