# Region — Sicilia

## Identity

Sicily is the grain and estate region. Its missions show how food supply, debt, contracts, labor, and shipping sustain political power.

## Fixed hubs

Syracuse, Agrigentum, Lilybaeum, inland grain estates, coastal warehouses.

## Gameplay identity

Contract sabotage, grain logistics, coastal navigation, rural stealth, estate labor, and food shortages.

## Languages

Primary: Greek and Latin. Secondary: local Sicilian varieties and Punic commercial influence.

## Historical classification

Cities, grain importance, and Mediterranean trade are FACT/RECONSTRUCTION. Dama's contacts, estates, and missions are FICTION.
