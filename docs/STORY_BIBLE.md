# Story Bible

## Narrative promise

The player experiences the transformation of one man's escape into a collective campaign. The story is about ownership of bodies, ownership of information, and ownership of the future.

## Act themes

- Act I: A person can be legally owned and still possess expertise, memory, and will.
- Act II: Escape routes are networks, not miracles.
- Act III: Law and commerce can be weapons as effective as soldiers.
- Act IV: Destruction is only liberation if people have somewhere to go.

## Canonical emotional beats

1. Dama watches Livia leave and cannot intervene without killing everyone nearby.
2. Dama chooses to return for the ledger rather than flee immediately.
3. Livia rejects the idea that she was merely waiting to be rescued.
4. Eirene forces Dama to prioritize evacuation over revenge.
5. Dama defeats Vindex only after the archive has served the rescue operation.
6. The epilogue refuses a false clean victory.
