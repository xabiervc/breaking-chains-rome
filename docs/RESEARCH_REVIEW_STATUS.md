## Current evidence level

The repository contains a source-ledger-backed research package covering Roman slavery, urbanism, the Great Fire, travel, language, material culture, sensitivity, accessibility, art, and audio.

## Confidence policy

Claims are classified as FACT, RECONSTRUCTION, FICTION, or OPEN QUESTION. High confidence means strong support for the stated claim; it does not mean every detail of the game is historically proven.
