# Exposition and Content Density

## Principles

- Show systems through play before explaining them in prose.
- One scene should usually advance character, system, and historical context, but not repeat the same information in every channel.
- Codex entries are optional depth, never a substitute for clear immediate objectives.
- Contradictory testimony should be framed as uncertainty, not forced into one omniscient answer.
- Use environmental detail, dialogue, mechanics, and consequence as distinct channels.

## Redundancy budget

A critical fact may have one primary presentation and up to two reinforcing presentations. Repetition beyond that requires a new gameplay purpose or is removed. Every mission brief records which channel owns each fact.

## Exposition QA

Testers record whether they can identify objective, stakes, uncertainty, and consequence without reading optional codex material.
