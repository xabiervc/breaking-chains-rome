# Progression and Economy

## Progression philosophy

Power comes from competence, knowledge, allies, and infrastructure. Money helps but cannot replace trust, language, evidence, or shelter.

## Currency and resources

Track denarii, food units, medicine units, metal stock, cloth, tools, documents, safe-house capacity, and transport capacity. Each resource has a fixed source list and consumption table.

## Reputation groups

- Enslaved communities.
- Freed communities.
- Merchants.
- Roman authorities.
- Resistance leadership.

## Economy principles

Prices are regional and date-sensitive but deterministic. War, shortage, lockdown, and route control alter documented modifiers. The economy must never reward exploitation of enslaved people as a neutral optimization strategy.

## Progression gates

Main regions unlock through story routes, evidence, language, money, or allies. Side content increases options and reduces human cost but cannot bypass canonical narrative requirements.
