# Breaking Chains: Rome — Master Game Design Document

## 1. High concept

A mature open-world action-adventure about an enslaved Thracian blacksmith who transforms a personal escape into a ten-year campaign against an imperial trafficking network, culminating in a fictionalized initiation of the Great Fire of Rome.

## 2. Design pillars

1. Freedom is logistical, emotional, legal, and collective.
2. The empire is a connected system rather than a backdrop.
3. Every region provides a distinct form of play.
4. Revenge escalates into organized liberation.
5. Historical uncertainty is acknowledged rather than hidden.
6. Deterministic content enables consistent writing, testing, and AI-assisted development.

## 3. Canon

- Time: AD 54–64.
- Opening: Ashgrove Estate near Capua.
- Final mission: Rome, 18 July AD 64.
- Protagonist: Dama.
- Sister: Livia.
- Personal antagonist: Vindex Varro.
- Network targets: Aulus Secundus, Gaius Valerius Crispus, Marcus Laelius Drusus, Lucius Fabius Varro.
- Canonical climax: The Great Fire.

## 4. Campaign

### Act I — The Iron Estate

Dama discovers the estate's trafficking records, loses Livia to sale, escapes with three companions, reaches the Alban Hills, and kills overseer Herennius.

### Act II — The Empire Hunts

Dama crosses Rome, Ostia, Sicily, Alexandria, Antioch, Thrace, Hispania Baetica, and Gallia Narbonensis. He develops language, disguise, investigation, travel, and recruitment skills. He discovers four protected network centers.

### Act III — The Four Debts

Four target arcs contain three investigations plus a fixed resolution each. The shipping magnate is exposed; the governor is removed; the military procurator is captured after a mutiny; the senator escapes conviction but reveals the need to destroy Vindex's archive.

### Act IV — Rome in Flames

Dama prepares evacuation routes, recruits six specialists, inventories supplies, retrieves the archive, ignites a warehouse on 18 July AD 64, frees 40 captives, defeats Vindex, and escapes through the Cloaca Maxima.

## 5. World

The 300-square-mile gameplay abstraction contains Italia, Sicilia, Graecia, Thrace, Asia Minor, Syria, Aegyptus, Africa Proconsularis, Hispania, and Gallia Narbonensis. Roads, rivers, ports, and sea lanes form a persistent travel network.

## 6. Languages

Voice acting is in historically researched in-universe languages: Classical Latin, Koine Greek, Western Aramaic, Egyptian, Punic, regional Iberian, Gaulish, and cautiously reconstructed Thracian. Interface/subtitles support English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Korean.

## 7. Core systems

- Exploration and route discovery.
- Stealth and identity management.
- Forging, crafting, repair, and sabotage.
- Language proficiency.
- Reputation across five groups.
- Recruitment and resistance logistics.
- Rescue ledger with real support costs.
- Deterministic economy and travel.
- Historical fire and evacuation simulation.

## 8. Production standard

No new content is canonical until it has stable IDs, explicit prerequisites, dates, locations, historical classification, deterministic behavior, and cross-document validation.
